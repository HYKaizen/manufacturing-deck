**DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS**

With every shipment, you must provide:

- ☐ **Certificate of Analysis (COA)** — confirming CTQ characteristics are within specification
- ☐ **Lot / Batch number** — clearly marked on packaging and COA
- ☐ **Quantity confirmation** — matching purchase order quantity
- ☐ **Applicable certifications** (material certs, safety certs, etc.)
- ☐ **Revision level** of drawings / specifications used

---

**PACKAGING & LABELING REQUIREMENTS**

- Parts must be packaged to prevent damage during transit
- Each package must be clearly labeled with: Part Name, Part Number, Lot/Batch Number, Quantity, Date of Manufacture
- Escape Trailer PO number must appear on all shipping documents

---

**NON-CONFORMANCE POLICY**

In the event that supplied material does not meet the above requirements:

- Escape Trailer will issue a **Non-Conformance Notice (NCN)** within 1 business day of detection
- Supplier must acknowledge within **1 business day**
- Supplier must provide containment actions within **2 business days**
- A **Corrective Action Request (CAR)** may be issued for repeat or critical issues

---

**SUPPLIER ACKNOWLEDGEMENT**

Please sign and return this letter within **5 business days** to confirm your understanding and commitment to these requirements.

By signing, your organization confirms that:
1. You have reviewed and understood all CTQ requirements listed above
2. Your current process is capable of meeting these requirements
3. You will notify Escape Trailer Quality immediately if any issue arises that may affect your ability to meet these requirements
