## 📊 **SUMMARY SCORING**

### ✅ **Current System Wins:**
- **User-friendly operation** (no opening sequence issues)
- **Easy installation** (no complex brackets)
- **Blind system integration** (critical for your needs)
- **Maintenance accessibility** (struts and blinds)
- **Handle ergonomics** (bigger, better grip)

### ✅ **Alternative Sample Wins:**
- **Security** (locking mechanism)
- **Installation accuracy** (proper hole alignment)
- **Hardware durability** (metal vs plastic components)
- **Condensation management** (drainage plug)

### ❌ **Alternative Sample Major Issues:**
1. **Blind maintenance nightmare** - rollers inaccessible
2. **Complex installation** - time consuming brackets
3. **Opening sequence problem** - must fully open before close
4. **Lock mechanism issues** - gets stuck, hard to operate

---

## 🎯 **RECOMMENDATION**

**Current Assessment:** The alternative sample addresses some issues but creates bigger problems.

### **Critical Issues with Alternative:**
- **Blind integration is worse** - this is a deal-breaker for your application
- **Installation complexity** increases production time/cost
- **Operational sequence problems** will frustrate customers

### **What to Look For in Future Samples:**
1. **Hybrid approach** - current blind system + alternative's locking mechanism
2. **Better hole alignment** without complex brackets
3. **Metal catches** to replace plastic components
4. **Maintain current installation simplicity**

### **Verdict:**
**REJECT Alternative Sample** - Despite security improvements, the blind integration and operational issues outweigh benefits.

**Next Steps:**
1. Request samples that maintain current blind system
2. Focus on improving handle mechanism with locking
3. Specify metal catch components
4. Require precise hole alignment without brackets
