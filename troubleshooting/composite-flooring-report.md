# Composite Panel Flooring — Research Report
**Prepared by:** Hakan Yasar | Travel Trailer Manufacturing, Chilliwack BC  
**Date:** February 2026

## 1. Executive Summary

This report evaluates composite sandwich panel materials as a replacement for traditional plywood/wood flooring in travel trailer manufacturing. Composite panels offer significant advantages in moisture resistance, weight reduction, and long-term durability. Industry leaders including Airstream, Oliver Travel Trailers, and Casita already use composite construction, and the technology is proven across transit bus fleets in North America.

---

## 2. Why Replace Wood Flooring?

| Issue with Wood | Impact |
|---|---|
| Moisture absorption | Rot, delamination, mold over time |
| Weight | Higher tow vehicle fuel consumption |
| Dimensional instability | Warping, squeaking, fastener loosening |
| Maintenance | Costly warranty repairs |
| Lifespan | Shorter product lifespan vs composite |

---

## 3. Composite Flooring Options

### 3.1 Honeycomb Sandwich Panels ⭐ Recommended
**Structure:** FRP or aluminum skins + PP (polypropylene) or aluminum honeycomb core

- Highest strength-to-weight ratio of all options
- 100% moisture-proof
- Typical weight: 4–8 kg/m² (vs 15–20 kg/m² for 19mm plywood)
- Used in transit buses (New Flyer, Proterra), aerospace, and premium RVs
- Typical floor spec: FRP skins 0.8–1.5mm + PP honeycomb core 20–30mm

**Pros:** Extremely lightweight, no rot/mold, long service life, industry proven  
**Cons:** Requires special fastener hardware (see Section 5)

---

### 3.2 Foam Core Sandwich Panels (FRP/CFRT skins + foam core)
**Core options:** XPS, PU, PET, PVC foam

- Good thermal insulation (important for Canadian climate)
- Slightly heavier than honeycomb, but better insulating
- More impact resistance than honeycomb in thin panels
- Easier to cut with standard tools

**Pros:** Built-in insulation, good moisture resistance, easy to work with  
**Cons:** Heavier than honeycomb, less stiff per unit weight

---

### 3.3 CFRT (Continuous Fiber Reinforced Thermoplastic) Panels
- Newer technology — glass or carbon fiber embedded in thermoplastic matrix
- Very high strength, recyclable, impact resistant
- Higher cost, more specialized tooling required
- Best for high-end/lightweight premium trailers

## 5. Installation & Hardware

### 5.1 The Challenge
Standard wood screws do not hold in honeycomb panels — the hollow core provides no thread engagement.

### 5.2 Recommended Connection System

**For floor panel → wood wall/frame connection:**

**Step 1 — Pre-embedded Edge Frame**
- Request supplier to embed aluminum channel or wood blocking strip around the panel perimeter during manufacturing
- No cost premium — part of panel spec
- Allows standard screws at panel edges, just like wood

**Step 2 — Aluminum L-Profile Extrusion**
- Screw aluminum angle bracket to existing wood wall or separation panel
- Composite floor panel rests on and bonds into the angle
- Profile: 40×40mm or 50×30mm aluminum angle, 2–3mm wall thickness

**Step 3 — Structural Adhesive**
- Bond panel-to-frame joint with SikaFlex 252 or SikaFlex 552
- Polyurethane-based, flexible when cured (handles trailer vibration)
- Apply to contact surfaces before setting panel in place

**Step 4 — Mechanical Fastener (optional but recommended)**
- Rivnuts or potted inserts through panel skin into embedded frame
- Provides secondary retention if adhesive is ever compromised

### 5.3 Fastener Types

| Type | Best For | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Potted Insert | High-load points, structural | Most pull-out strength |
| Rivnut (Rivet Nut) | Medium loads, quick install | One-tool installation |
| Pre-embedded wood block | Edge fastening, perimeter | Simplest for production line |
| Structural adhesive (SikaFlex 252) | Panel-to-frame bond | Flexible, vibration-resistant |

## 7. Recommended Specification (Floor Panel)

| Parameter | Specification |
|---|---|
| Panel type | Honeycomb sandwich |
| Face sheets | FRP (fiberglass reinforced plastic), 1.0–1.5mm |
| Core | PP (polypropylene) honeycomb, 25mm |
| Total thickness | ~27mm |
| Panel density | ~5–7 kg/m² |
| Edge treatment | Pre-embedded aluminum channel (perimeter) |
| Surface finish | Anti-slip texture or smooth (for vinyl flooring overlay) |
| Connection to structure | Aluminum L-profile + SikaFlex 252 adhesive |
