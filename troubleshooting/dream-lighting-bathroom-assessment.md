## 1. Executive Summary

This report evaluates whether the **Dream Lighting P/N 0016048CW** (12V DC, 3.9W oval LED dome light with motion sensor) is suitable for use in RV bathroom environments, including wet shower areas.

**Conclusion:** The fixture is **approved for general RV bathroom ceiling use** but is **NOT recommended for installation inside or directly above shower enclosures** due to its damp-location-only rating. Installing it in a wet zone would void its certification, create safety and durability risks, and potentially expose Escape Trailer to liability.

## 4. Understanding Damp vs. Wet Locations

### 4.1 Regulatory Definitions

The Canadian Electrical Code (CEC) and National Electrical Code (NEC) define three installation environments:

| Classification | Definition | Examples in an RV Bathroom |
|---|---|---|
| **Dry Location** | Not normally subject to moisture | Bedroom, living area |
| **Damp Location** | Subject to moderate degrees of moisture — condensation, humidity, occasional water vapor | General bathroom ceiling, vanity area, toilet area |
| **Wet Location** | Subject to saturation with water — direct spray, splashing, running water | Inside shower enclosure, directly above showerhead, within shower spray zone |

### 4.2 Rough Equivalence to IP Codes

For reference purposes (IP codes are not required in North America):

| CSA/NEC Rating | Approximate IP Equivalent | Protection Level |
|---|---|---|
| Damp Location | IP21 — IP23 | Protected against dripping water and condensation |
| Wet Location | IP44 — IP65 | Protected against splashing water from any direction |

### 4.3 Shower Zone Definition (NFPA 1192 / CEC)

The "wet zone" in an RV bathroom is typically defined as:

- **Zone 0:** Inside the shower tray / tub — submersion risk
- **Zone 1:** Directly above the shower tray to a height of 2.25m (7.4 ft) — heavy spray
- **Zone 2:** Extending 0.6m (2 ft) horizontally from the edge of Zone 1 — splash risk

The Dream Lighting 0016048CW is rated for areas **outside** Zones 0, 1, and 2 only.

## 6. Assessment: Inside Shower Enclosure (WET Zone)

### ❌ NOT RECOMMENDED — Do Not Install

| Factor | Risk Level | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| **Safety** | ⚠️ Medium | While 12V DC significantly reduces shock risk compared to 120V AC, water intrusion into the fixture can cause: short circuits in the LED driver, corrosion of electrical contacts, potential arcing at connection points, and motion sensor malfunction (false triggers or failure to activate). |
| **Durability** | 🔴 High Risk | Direct water spray will cause premature failure. Specific failure modes include: **water ingress** through unsealed lens gaskets (damp-rated seals are not designed for pressurized spray), **LED driver failure** from moisture reaching PCB components, **corrosion** of internal wiring connections and solder joints, **lens fogging/discoloration** from repeated water and soap residue exposure, **motion sensor degradation** from water film on the PIR lens. Expected failure timeline: 3–12 months in a shower environment vs. 5+ years rated life in proper damp installation. |
| **Regulatory Compliance** | 🔴 Non-Compliant | NFPA 1192, CEC, and CSA standards **require wet-location-rated fixtures** in shower enclosures. Installing a damp-only fixture in a wet zone is a **code violation**. This would be flagged during: CSA compliance audits, RVIA inspections, insurance claim investigations, and any third-party quality assessment. |
| **Warranty** | 🔴 Voided | Installation outside the product's rated environment (damp vs. wet) voids the manufacturer warranty. QAI certification applies **only** when installed per label instructions. |
| **Insurance & Liability** | 🔴 Critical Risk | If a fixture failure in a shower zone causes damage or injury, Escape Trailer's product liability insurance could be jeopardized. The non-compliant installation would be clear evidence of failure to follow applicable codes. This is a **significant legal exposure** for a manufacturer. |
| **Customer Perception** | ⚠️ Medium | Premature light failure in a shower (even without safety incident) generates warranty claims, negative reviews, and service calls — all avoidable with correct fixture selection. |

### 6.1 Specific Failure Scenarios in Shower Use

1. **Water pooling inside fixture** → LED driver short circuit → light failure → customer service call
2. **Corrosion at wire connections** → intermittent operation → potential arcing → fire risk (very low at 12V but not zero)
3. **Motion sensor water film** → erratic behavior (light stays on/off randomly) → customer complaint
4. **Soap/shampoo residue** → lens discoloration → reduced light output → appearance issue
5. **Condensation cycling** → internal moisture trapped → mold growth inside fixture → health/appearance concern

## 9. Conclusion

The Dream Lighting P/N 0016048CW is an excellent, fully certified choice for general RV bathroom ceiling installation. Its 12V DC operation, CSA C22.2 No. 250.0 certification, and built-in motion sensor make it well-suited for the bathroom environment outside the shower zone.

However, it must **not** be installed in the shower enclosure or any area subject to direct water spray. Doing so would violate NFPA 1192 and CSA standards, void the product warranty, create premature failure risk, and expose Escape Trailer Industries to regulatory non-compliance and product liability.

**Action Required:** Source a separate CSA wet-location-rated 12V LED fixture for shower enclosure installation.
