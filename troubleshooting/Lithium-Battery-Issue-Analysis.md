## TECHNICAL ROOT CAUSE

### **Battery Behavior Problem:**
```
Issue: Lithium batteries indicating "fully charged" when they are not
Result: Power fluctuations and surges during cycling
Impact: Electrical system instability and customer complaints
Scope: "Likely all of them" - fleet-wide issue suspected
```

### **Why This Happens:**
- **Battery Management System (BMS) inaccuracy**
- **State of Charge (SOC) drift** over time  
- **Voltage vs. actual capacity mismatch**
- **Industry-known lithium behavior** (not Escape-specific)

---

## PROPOSED SOLUTIONS

### **Primary Solution: Battery Calibration Protocol**
```
Frequency: Every 2-4 weeks
Process: Full charge cycle to reset battery management system
Status: Victron best practice recommendation
Communication: Must be communicated to all lithium customers
```

### **Secondary Solution: Battery Bank Increase**
```
Purpose: Provide capacity buffer during SOC inaccuracies
Need: Not technically necessary but customer preference option
Cost: Additional battery investment per unit
Benefit: Reduced customer complaints, improved reliability margin
```
