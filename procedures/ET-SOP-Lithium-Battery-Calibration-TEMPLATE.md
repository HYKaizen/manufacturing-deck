## 1. PURPOSE & SCOPE

### 1.1 Purpose
Standard procedure for lithium battery State of Charge (SOC) calibration to resolve power fluctuation issues caused by Battery Management System (BMS) drift.

### 1.2 Scope
- **Applies to:** All Escape Trailers with lithium battery systems
- **Frequency:** Every 2-4 weeks (exact frequency: [TO BE CONFIRMED])
- **Users:** Customers (primary) and Service Technicians (diagnostic)

### 1.3 Background
Lithium batteries experience SOC drift where the BMS shows "fully charged" when actual capacity is lower, causing power fluctuations during electrical cycling.
