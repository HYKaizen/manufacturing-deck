## 📋 **CONCLUSION BASED ON PHOTO EVIDENCE**

The photographic evidence clearly shows that while the alternative system addresses some minor issues, it creates major functional problems that are unacceptable:

1. **Photo 10 is the deal-breaker** - blind maintenance is impossible
2. **Photos 1, 4, 12 show operational problems** that will frustrate customers
3. **Photo 7 shows installation complexity** that increases production costs

**The current system's advantages (Photos 2, 4, 6) far outweigh its disadvantages.**

**Recommendation:** Use photo evidence to communicate specific requirements to suppliers - improve current system incrementally rather than complete redesign.
