# Escape Trailer - Wood Panel Quality Standards
**Document:** ET-QS-005  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** March 4, 2026  
**Owner:** Hakan Yasar, Director of Quality  

## Scope
Quality standards for wood panel cutting, CNC operations, and subassembly components used in Escape Trailer manufacturing.

---

## 1. CUTTING STANDARDS

### 1.1 Dimensional Tolerance
- **Linear dimensions:** ±1.5mm (±1/16") 
- **CNC cut parts:** ±0.5mm (±0.020")
- **Critical assembly interfaces:** ±0.8mm (±1/32")

### 1.2 Edge Quality
**Manual/Table Saw Cuts:**
- No tear-out exceeding 3mm (1/8") deep
- Clean, straight cuts with minimal blade marks
- No splintering on visible edges

**CNC Router Cuts:**
- Edge smoothness: 125 Ra max (3.2μm)
- No chipping or tear-out on finished edges
- Corner radii: As specified ±0.2mm

### 1.3 Cut Angles
- **90° cuts:** ±1° tolerance
- **Specified angles:** ±0.5° tolerance  
- **Beveled edges:** Per drawing specification ±0.5°

---

## 2. FLATNESS STANDARDS

### 2.1 Panel Flatness
- **Standard panels:** 2mm deviation max over 1m span
- **Critical surfaces:** 1mm deviation max over 1m span
- **Measurement method:** Straight edge and feeler gauge

### 2.2 Twist/Warp
- **Acceptable twist:** <2mm over panel diagonal
- **Local deformation:** <1mm over 300mm span

---

## 3. SURFACE DEFECT STANDARDS

### 3.1 Visible Surfaces (Class A)
**REJECT if present:**
- Gouges, scratches, or dents >0.5mm deep
- Burn marks from cutting/routing
- Adhesive residue or staining
- Exposed core material on veneer edges

**ACCEPTABLE (with limits):**
- Minor grain variation (natural wood characteristic)
- Tool marks <0.2mm deep on non-visible areas
- Small knots if structurally sound and <10mm diameter

### 3.2 Hidden/Structural Surfaces (Class B)
**REJECT if present:**
- Structural damage affecting strength
- Delamination >25mm length
- Through-holes not per specification
- Cracks extending >50mm

**ACCEPTABLE:**
- Minor surface imperfections not affecting function
- Tool marks on hidden surfaces
- Natural wood variations

---

## 4. CNC SPECIFIC STANDARDS

### 4.1 Routing Quality
- **Feed rate marks:** Minimal, <0.1mm depth
- **Corner quality:** Sharp internal corners where specified
- **Surface finish:** 125 Ra (3.2μm) on finished surfaces

### 4.2 Hole Specifications
- **Diameter tolerance:** +0.1/-0.0mm for fastener holes
- **Position tolerance:** ±0.3mm from specified center
- **Edge distance:** Per drawing, minimum 2x hole diameter

### 4.3 Profile Accuracy
- **Contoured edges:** ±0.5mm from CAD specification
- **Notches/cutouts:** ±0.3mm dimensional tolerance
- **Radii:** ±0.2mm from specified radius

---

## 5. SUBASSEMBLY STANDARDS

### 5.1 Fit & Finish
- **Gap tolerance:** 1-3mm for design gaps, 0-1mm for tight joints
- **Flush alignment:** ±0.5mm deviation at joint interfaces
- **Fastener engagement:** Minimum 75% thread engagement

### 5.2 Adhesive Joints
- **Squeeze-out:** Clean, consistent bead with no voids
- **Clamp pressure:** Per adhesive manufacturer specification
- **Cure time:** Full specification before handling

---

## 6. INSPECTION METHODS

### 6.1 Required Tools
- Steel rulers (300mm, 1000mm)
- Feeler gauges (0.05-3.0mm set)
- Digital calipers (±0.02mm accuracy)
- Straight edge (1000mm minimum)
- Go/no-go gauges for critical dimensions

### 6.2 Sampling Plan
- **Production lots <50 pieces:** 100% inspection
- **Production lots >50 pieces:** Statistical sampling per AQL 2.5
- **First article:** Full dimensional inspection required

---

## 7. NON-CONFORMANCE

### 7.1 Rework Criteria
**REWORK if possible:**
- Dimensions outside tolerance but material removal viable
- Edge defects correctable with sanding/routing
- Surface defects correctable with approved methods

### 7.2 Rejection Criteria
**SCRAP - No rework:**
- Structural damage or delamination
- Dimensional errors requiring material addition
- Critical interface dimensions outside tolerance

---

## 8. DOCUMENTATION

### 8.1 Required Records
- First article inspection sheets
- In-process inspection logs
- Non-conformance reports
- Corrective action tracking

### 8.2 Retention Period
- Quality records: 7 years minimum
- First article reports: Life of part/drawing
- NC reports: 5 years minimum
