## 1. PURPOSE & SCOPE

### 1.1 Purpose
This document establishes the Supplier Quality System (SQS) framework for Escape Trailer Industries Ltd. It defines the requirements, processes, and communication routes for ensuring that all supplied materials, components, and services meet Escape Trailer's quality standards before reaching the production line.

### 1.2 Scope
This framework applies to:
- All direct material suppliers (raw materials, components, assemblies)
- All indirect suppliers providing services that affect product quality
- New supplier qualification and onboarding
- Ongoing supplier performance management

### 1.3 Objectives
- Prevent quality failures at source (supplier level)
- Define and communicate Critical to Quality (CTQ) requirements clearly
- Establish a structured non-conformance response process
- Monitor and improve supplier performance continuously

## 2. SUPPLIER QUALIFICATION PROCESS

### 2.1 New Supplier Approval

All new suppliers must complete the following steps before receiving a purchase order:

**Step 1 — Supplier Self-Assessment**
- Supplier completes the Escape Trailer Supplier Self-Assessment Questionnaire
- Documents required: Quality certifications (ISO 9001, IATF, etc.), business registration, insurance, product samples

**Step 2 — Document Review**
- Quality team reviews submitted documents
- Minimum requirement: documented quality management system in place
- Review turnaround: 5 business days

**Step 3 — Initial Sample Inspection (ISI)**
- Supplier submits 3–5 samples of each part/material
- Escape Trailer Quality inspects against drawings and specifications
- Pass/Fail result communicated within 5 business days

**Step 4 — Trial Order**
- Approved suppliers receive a trial purchase order (minimum 1 shipment)
- Full incoming inspection applied to trial orders
- Performance evaluated before full approval

**Step 5 — Supplier Approval**
- Supplier added to Approved Supplier List (ASL)
- CTQ Requirements Letter issued (see Communication Templates)
- Supplier assigned performance tier: Preferred / Approved / Conditional

### 2.2 Approved Supplier List (ASL)
- Maintained by Quality Department
- Reviewed and updated quarterly
- Purchase orders may only be issued to ASL-listed suppliers

## 3. CTQ REQUIREMENTS DEFINITION & COMMUNICATION

### 3.1 What is a CTQ?
A Critical to Quality (CTQ) characteristic is any product or process parameter whose variation has a direct and significant impact on Escape Trailer product quality, safety, or customer satisfaction.

### 3.2 CTQ Identification Process

| Step | Action | Responsible |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Review customer complaints and warranty data | Quality |
| 2 | Review design drawings and specifications | Engineering |
| 3 | Identify key dimensions, materials, finishes | Quality + Engineering |
| 4 | Define measurement method and acceptable tolerance | Quality |
| 5 | Document CTQs in the Part Specification Sheet | Quality |

### 3.3 CTQ Communication to Suppliers
- CTQ requirements communicated via **CTQ Requirements Letter** at supplier onboarding
- Updated CTQs communicated within 5 business days of change
- Suppliers must acknowledge receipt and confirm compliance capability
- Supplier must provide Certificate of Analysis (COA) or dimensional report for CTQ characteristics on each shipment

## 4. INCOMING INSPECTION PROCESS

### 4.1 Inspection Levels

| Supplier Tier | Inspection Level | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Preferred | Reduced | Visual check + COA review only |
| Approved | Normal | Sample inspection per AQL 1.0 |
| Conditional | Tightened | 100% inspection of all received parts |
| New Supplier | Full | 100% inspection for first 3 shipments |

### 4.2 Inspection Procedure
1. Receiving team logs incoming shipment in system
2. Quality holds shipment — do not move to stock until released
3. Inspector checks quantity, labeling, packaging condition
4. Inspector verifies COA / documentation against requirements
5. Inspector performs dimensional / visual inspection per sampling plan
6. Pass → Receiving stamp applied, material released to stock
7. Fail → Non-Conformance Notice issued (see Section 5)

### 4.3 Documentation
- Incoming Inspection Record completed for every shipment
- Records retained for minimum 3 years

## 5. NON-CONFORMANCE HANDLING

### 5.1 Detection & Tagging
- Non-conforming material identified with a RED "HOLD" tag immediately upon detection
- Material quarantined in designated hold area — not to be used in production

### 5.2 Non-Conformance Notice (NCN)
- Quality issues a Non-Conformance Notice (NCN) to the supplier within 1 business day
- NCN includes: description of defect, quantity affected, photos, required supplier response date

### 5.3 Disposition Options

| Disposition | Condition | Approval Required |
|---|---|---|
| Return to Supplier (RTS) | Parts not usable | Quality Manager |
| Sort & Use | Some parts acceptable | Quality |
| Rework at Escape | Minor, fixable defects | Quality + Operations |
| Use As Is (UAI) | Non-critical deviation | Quality + Engineering |
| Scrap | Beyond repair | Quality Manager |

### 5.4 Response Requirements
- Supplier must acknowledge NCN within **1 business day**
- Supplier must provide containment actions within **2 business days**
- Full Corrective Action Request (CAR) response required within **10 business days**

## 6. CORRECTIVE ACTION REQUEST (CAR) PROCESS

### 6.1 When a CAR is Issued
- Repeat non-conformance (same defect twice in 6 months)
- Critical defect affecting safety or function
- High-volume rejection (>5% of shipment rejected)

### 6.2 CAR Process Flow

```
Issue CAR → Supplier Containment (2 days) → Root Cause Analysis (10 days)
→ Corrective Action Plan → Escape Verification → Close or Escalate
```

### 6.3 Root Cause Tools
Suppliers are required to use structured root cause analysis:
- **5-Why Analysis** (minimum requirement)
- **Fishbone / Ishikawa Diagram** (for complex issues)
- **8D Report** (for critical or systemic issues)

### 6.4 Escalation
- CAR not responded to within 10 days → Supplier placed on Conditional status
- Repeat CAR within 6 months → Supplier review meeting required
- 3+ open CARs → Supplier placed on probation / potential disqualification

## 7. SUPPLIER PERFORMANCE MONITORING

### 7.1 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Target | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Lot Acceptance Rate (LAR) | ≥ 98% | Accepted lots / total lots received |
| On-Time Delivery (OTD) | ≥ 95% | Shipments on time / total shipments |
| NCN Rate | ≤ 2 per quarter | Number of NCNs issued |
| CAR Response Rate | 100% within 10 days | CARs responded / CARs issued |
| Corrective Action Effectiveness | ≥ 90% | CARs closed without repeat issue |

### 7.2 Performance Review Frequency

| Supplier Tier | Review Frequency |
|---|---|
| Preferred | Annually |
| Approved | Semi-annually |
| Conditional | Quarterly |

### 7.3 Supplier Scorecard
- Formal scorecard completed at each review cycle
- Scorecard shared with supplier
- Improvement targets set for underperforming categories
- Tier reassignment based on scorecard results

## 8. COMMUNICATION ROUTES & ESCALATION

### 8.1 Standard Communication Route

```
Supplier → Escape Purchasing → Quality Department
```

### 8.2 Quality Department Contacts (Internal)

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Director of Quality, CI and PDI (Hakan Yasar) | SQS owner, CAR review, supplier decisions |
| Purchasing | PO management, supplier commercial relationship |
| Receiving / Inspection | Incoming inspection, NCN initiation |
| Engineering | CTQ definition, use-as-is decisions |

### 8.3 Escalation Path

| Issue Level | Action | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| Minor NCN | Quality Inspector handles | Same day |
| Repeated NCN | Quality Lead issues CAR | Within 2 days |
| Supplier non-responsive | Quality Lead contacts supplier management | Within 5 days |
| Systemic supplier failure | Quality Lead + Purchasing review supplier status | Within 10 days |
