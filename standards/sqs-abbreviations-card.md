# Supplier Quality System — Abbreviations & Terms

## Escape Trailer Industries Ltd.

| Abbreviation | Full Term | What It Means |
|:---:|:---|:---|
| **SQS** | Supplier Quality System | The overall framework governing how Escape Trailer manages supplier quality (ET-SQS-001/002/003) |
| **CTQ** | Critical to Quality | Any product or process parameter whose variation directly impacts product quality, safety, or customer satisfaction. CTQs are communicated to suppliers and must be verified on every shipment |
| **COA** | Certificate of Analysis | Document from the supplier certifying that a shipment meets specified requirements. Must accompany every shipment for CTQ characteristics |
| **CAR** | Corrective Action Request | Formal request issued to a supplier when a quality problem requires root cause analysis and permanent corrective action. Supplier must respond within 10 business days |
| **NCN** | Non-Conformance Notice | Notice issued to a supplier within 1 business day when incoming material fails inspection. Includes defect description, quantity affected, photos, and response deadline |
| **ASL** | Approved Supplier List | Master list of suppliers authorized to receive purchase orders. Only ASL-listed suppliers may supply to Escape Trailer |
| **AQL** | Acceptable Quality Level | Statistical sampling standard used for incoming inspection. Levels vary by supplier tier (e.g., AQL 1.0 for Approved tier, AQL 0.65 for Conditional) |
| **OTD** | On-Time Delivery | KPI measuring the percentage of shipments delivered on or before the agreed date. Target: ≥ 95% |
| **LAR** | Lot Acceptance Rate | KPI measuring the percentage of received lots that pass incoming inspection. Target: ≥ 98% |
| **KPI** | Key Performance Indicator | Measurable metrics used to evaluate supplier performance (OTD, LAR, NCN Rate, CAR Response Rate, etc.) |
| **PO** | Purchase Order | Official order document issued to a supplier. May only be issued to ASL-listed suppliers |
| **BOM** | Bill of Materials | Complete list of parts, components, and materials needed to build a trailer or sub-assembly |
| **ETI** | Engineering Technical Information | Internal engineering reference number linked to BOM items for traceability |
| **QC** | Quality Control | Inspection and testing activities to verify products meet specifications |
| **ISO 9001** | International Organization for Standardization — Quality Management Systems | Internationally recognized quality management certification. Preferred for Escape Trailer suppliers |
| **IATF 16949** | International Automotive Task Force — Quality Management Standard | Automotive-specific quality management certification (builds on ISO 9001). Valued for supplier qualification |
| **PDI** | Pre-Delivery Inspection | Final inspection of a completed trailer before delivery to the customer |
| **CI** | Continuous Improvement | Ongoing effort to improve products, processes, or services through incremental and breakthrough changes |
| **ET** | Escape Trailer | Company abbreviation used in document numbering (e.g., ET-SQS-001) |
| **CM** | Change Management | Structured process for evaluating and implementing changes to products, components, or processes (ET-CIP-142) |
| **CIP** | Continuous Improvement Project | Tracked improvement project within Escape Trailer's CI program |
